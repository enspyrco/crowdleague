## Executive Summary

### CrowdLeague Funding Overview

CrowdLeague is positioned at the intersection of three major trends:
1. **Sports technology growth** - Australian sports tech market projected to reach AUD $2.16B by 2034 (13.1% CAGR)
2. **Youth sports participation crisis** - 34% of young Australians have asked to quit organized sports; 27% stop by age 15
3. **Brisbane 2032 Olympics investment** - $7.1B+ in infrastructure and grassroots programs

### Recommended Funding Strategy

| Phase | Target Amount | Primary Sources | Timeline |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|----------|
| **Pre-Seed** | $25,000-$50,000 | Government grants, ASF | 0-6 months |
| **Seed** | $100,000-$250,000 | Angels, Accelerators | 6-12 months |
| **Series A** | $500,000-$1,000,000 | VCs, Sports-focused funds | 12-24 months |

### Key Funding Targets

**Immediate Opportunities (High Fit):**
- Queensland Government Games On! Program
- Australian Sports Foundation (tax-deductible donations)
- River City Labs / Brisbane accelerators
- Brisbane Angels network

**Medium-Term Opportunities:**
- Startmate accelerator
- Sport Australia grants (via state body partnership)
- Brisbane 2032 legacy programs

### Federal (Australian Government)

#### Australian Sports Foundation (ASF)

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Administering Body** | Australian Sports Foundation |
| **Type** | Tax-deductible donation pathway |
| **Model** | 95% of donations distributed as grants |
| **Eligibility** | Athletes, coaches, clubs, schools, community groups |
| **Key Program** | Future Stars Grant (up to $100K for equipment) |

**Fit for CrowdLeague:** HIGH
- Can register project and receive tax-deductible donations
- Grassroots focus aligned perfectly
- No competitive grant process - based on fundraising

**How to Use:**
1. Register CrowdLeague as an ASF project
2. Donors receive tax deductions for contributions
3. ASF distributes 95% of donations to your project
4. Market to corporate sponsors seeking tax-deductible sports giving

#### Sport Australia - Play Well Grants

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|

## Brisbane 2032 Olympics Opportunities

### Overview

The Brisbane 2032 Olympics represents a once-in-a-generation opportunity for sports technology companies. The Queensland Government has committed to making this "the community's Games" with significant investment in grassroots sports.

### Key Programs

#### Elevate 2042 Legacy Strategy

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Vision** | 10-year legacy plan extending beyond 2032 |
| **Focus Areas** | Youth participation, community sports, health outcomes |
| **Alignment** | CrowdLeague directly addresses youth participation goals |

**CrowdLeague Positioning:**
- "Building the grassroots communities that create champions"
- Today's teenagers are tomorrow's Olympic athletes
- Digital infrastructure for community sports connection

#### Games On! Program

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Investment** | $250 million |
| **Purpose** | Grassroots club support across Queensland |
| **Streams** | Equipment, facilities, programs, technology |

**Approach:**
1. Position as technology partner for clubs receiving grants
2. Apply directly for innovation/technology stream
3. Partner with state sporting organizations

#### Go for Gold Fund

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Investment** | $100 million |
| **Purpose** | School sports infrastructure and programs |
| **Managed By** | Department of Education |

**Approach:**
- Partner with schools for sports discovery
- Connect school facilities to community players
- After-school and weekend venue utilization

#### Infrastructure Investment

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Total** | $7.1 billion |
| **New Venues** | Multiple across Brisbane, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast |
| **Upgrade Focus** | Existing community facilities |

**Opportunity for CrowdLeague:**
- Digital layer on top of new/upgraded venues
- Venue discovery for new facilities
- Community connection for new sports precincts

### Strategic Positioning

**Key Messages for 2032 Opportunities:**

1. **Legacy Technology Partner**
   - "CrowdLeague is the digital legacy of Brisbane 2032"
   - Platform that outlasts the Games
   - Connects Olympic venues with community use

2. **Youth Participation Solution**
   - Addresses 34% youth dropout rate
   - Makes sport social and fun
   - Grassroots-first approach

3. **Venue Utilization**
   - Maximize return on infrastructure investment
   - Connect facilities with players
   - Enable casual/pickup use of venues

4. **Economic Impact**
   - Local tech company benefiting from Olympics investment
   - Job creation in Queensland
   - Export potential to other Olympic cities

### Timeline Alignment

| Year | Olympics Phase | CrowdLeague Opportunity |
|------|----------------|------------------------|
| 2025-2026 | Early planning | Position for legacy programs |
| 2027-2029 | Venue construction | Partner with new facilities |
| 2030-2031 | Pre-Games ramp-up | Scale platform across Queensland |
| 2032 | Games year | Maximum visibility, showcase app |
| 2033-2042 | Legacy period | Sustain community connections |

- Job creation and economic development
- Accessibility and inclusion

- "Community sports participation"
- "Youth engagement"

- "Grassroots development"

- "Legacy infrastructure"

- Community partnerships
