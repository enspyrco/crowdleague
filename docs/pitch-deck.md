## Slide 2: The Problem

### Young Australians are dropping out of sport

- 34% of young Australians have asked their parents if they can quit organized sports; 27% stop by age 15 ([Allianz/PureProfile 2024](https://www.allianz.com.au/about-us/media-hub/aussie-kids-drop-out-of-sport.html))
- Teens stay in sport when it's **fun and social** - but time pressure, cost, and competitiveness drive them away ([Allianz 2024](https://www.allianz.com.au/about-us/media-hub/aussie-kids-drop-out-of-sport.html))
- Finding pickup games, connecting with players at your level, and discovering local venues is fragmented

**The gap:** No single platform connects grassroots players with venues and each other.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

### CrowdLeague connects sports players with local venues and each other**

- **Discover venues** - Find sports facilities on an interactive map
- **Build your crew** - Connect with players who share your interests
- **Coordinate play** - Message your crew, notify them when you're heading to a venue
- **Grassroots-first** - Designed for casual players, not elite athletes

## Appendix: Funding Research

### Australian Sports Foundation (ASF)

- **What:** Only organization where sports donations are tax-deductible
- **Eligibility:** Athletes, coaches, clubs, schools, community groups
- **Model:** 95% of online donations distributed as grants (5% retained for platform costs) ([ASF FAQ](https://asf.org.au/faq/does-the-sports-foundation-charge-any-fees))
- **Programs:** Future Stars Grant ($100K equipment to grassroots clubs)
- **Contact:** asf.org.au

### Sport Australia Grants

- **Play Well Grants:** $10.3M allocated, up to $40K per project ([Sport Australia](https://www.ausport.gov.au/playwell/news/entry/$10.3-million-for-projects-to-get-more-aussies-moving))
- **Note:** Individual clubs not directly eligible - must go through state/national bodies
- **Local Sporting Champions:** Individual athlete grants (Round 3 open until Jan 31, 2026)

### Queensland Government (Brisbane 2032)

- **Games On!:** $250M for grassroots clubs across Queensland
- **Go for Gold Fund:** $100M for school sports infrastructure
- **Contact:** Investigate via local council or state sports bodies

### Telstra Foundation

- **Connected Communities:** Up to $10K grants
- **Focus:** Rural/regional communities, digital capability
- **2025 Round:** Applications closed March 2025, next round TBD
- **Note:** Not accepting unsolicited applications

### Westpac Foundation

- **Status:** Redesigning programs for 2026
- **New Focus:** Literacy and numeracy (pivoting away from general community grants)
- **Thrive Community Grants:** $25K-$50K but requires DGR-1 status

### Key Requirements for Most Grants

- Incorporated organization or ABN
- DGR-1 status (for some)
- Partnership with state/national sporting body (for some)
- Audited financials (for larger grants)

## Tailoring Guide

### For Government Grants

**Key modifications:**

| Slide | Government Focus |
|-------|-----------------|
| Problem | Emphasize health crisis, youth mental health, community disconnection |
| Market | Focus on participation numbers, not revenue opportunity |
| Business Model | Emphasize sustainability and community benefit over profit |
| Funding Ask | Frame as "community outcomes" not "investment returns" |
| Vision | Strong Brisbane 2032 legacy language |

**Language to use:**
- "Community sports participation"
- "Youth engagement and wellbeing"
- "Health outcomes"
- "Digital inclusion"
- "Grassroots legacy infrastructure"

**Metrics to emphasize:**
- Expected participation increase (%)
- Number of communities/venues served
- Youth engagement rates
- Accessibility and inclusion features

---

### For Angel Investors

**Key modifications:**

| Slide | Investor Focus |
|-------|---------------|
| Market | Add TAM/SAM/SOM breakdown, comparable exits |
| Current Status | Emphasize engagement metrics, growth indicators |
| Business Model | Add unit economics projections, LTV/CAC estimates |
| Funding Ask | Specific runway, valuation context, milestone-based tranches |
| Vision | Exit opportunities, acquirer landscape |

**Language to use:**
- "Total addressable market"
- "Network effects"
- "Capital efficiency"
- "Exit opportunities"
- "Comparable valuations"

**Metrics to emphasize:**
- User growth rate (MoM)
- Engagement (DAU/MAU ratio)
- Revenue projections
- Retention rates

---

### For Accelerators (Startmate, River City Labs, etc.)

**Key modifications:**

| Slide | Accelerator Focus |
|-------|------------------|
| Problem | Add personal founder connection to the problem |
| Current Status | Show iteration speed, learning velocity |
| Team | Emphasize coachability, learning examples, hustle |
| Funding Ask | What you'll accomplish during the program |
| Vision | Ambitious but grounded in evidence |

**Language to use:**
- "Founder-market fit"
- "Learning velocity"
- "Iteration speed"
- "Mentorship leverage"

**Include:**
- Examples of pivots or learnings
- Speed of development (built in X weeks)
- Willingness to change direction
- Big vision with humble approach

## Presentation Tips

### Government Grants
- Lead with community impact
- Use "we" language (community partnership)
- Include letters of support from local clubs
- Show alignment with government strategy documents

### Angel Investors
- Lead with market size
- Be specific about metrics and milestones
- Know your unit economics (even if estimated)
- Have a clear "path to exit" story

### Accelerators
- Lead with founder story
- Show you can learn and iterate fast
- Be coachable in the room
- Demonstrate hustle and resourcefulness
